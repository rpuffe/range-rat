# Playtest scorecard

Every playthrough, each playtester scores its stretch of the game from 1 to 10 on the same metrics,
with one line of why. Same metrics every round so the trend is the signal, not the absolute number.

| Metric | What it asks |
|---|---|
| **Fun** | Is it a good time, minute to minute? |
| **Clarity** | Do you always know what to do next, without reading paragraphs? |
| **Text load** | One message at a time, ~8 words (10 = never overwhelmed) |
| **Pacing** | Not too slow, not too fast; new things arrive at the right moments |
| **Co-op** | Does a crew of 4 naturally split up, help each other and shout at each other? |
| **Meaningful features** | Is every feature worth doing (10 = nothing feels like filler) |
| **Look & feel** | Graphics, physicality, juice, sound |
| **Polish** | Bugs, glitches, things that feel broken (10 = none seen) |
| **One more shift** | Do you want to keep playing? |
| **Overall** | The vibe |

Playtesters: **A** = a new crew's first night to the Range Club; **B** = the Club and Tiered;
**C** = Stadium → County → Orbital → the Last Bucket → endless.

## Round 2 (2026-11-24, build d66602a: council round 6 built)

Fresh playtesters, same slices and metrics. Full reports: design/playtest-round2.md.

| Metric | A | B | C | **Avg** | Round 1 avg | Δ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fun | 6 | 6.5 | 6.5 | **6.3** | 6.0 | +0.3 |
| Clarity | 6 | 5 | 7 | **6.0** | 5.7 | +0.3 |
| Text load | 5 | 5 | 5 | **5.0** | 4.0 | +1.0 |
| Pacing | 6 | 7 | 6.5 | **6.5** | 5.0 | +1.5 |
| Co-op | 6 | 6 | 6 | **6.0** | 6.0 | 0 |
| Meaningful features | 6 | 6 | 5.5 | **5.8** | 4.7 | +1.1 |
| Look & feel | 7 | 7 | 7.5 | **7.2** | 7.0 | +0.2 |
| Polish | 6 | 5.5 | 6 | **5.8** | 4.7 | +1.1 |
| One more shift | 7 | 6.5 | 5 | **6.2** | 6.0 | +0.2 |
| **Overall** | **6.5** | **6** | **6** | **6.2** | 5.5 | **+0.7** |

**Read:** every metric rose or held. Pacing (+1.5), Meaningful features (+1.1), Polish (+1.1) and Text
load (+1.0) moved most: earned stamps, the gauge and the bell gave each day a goal. Co-op didn't move:
the crew still splits only at the bell. Text load is still the lowest score (5): every tester saw two or
three centre texts at once at the bell or the finale. Biggest drags: the rally pointing at the wrong
thing (A), gutters switching off Tiered's greens (B), endless having nothing to do (C).
