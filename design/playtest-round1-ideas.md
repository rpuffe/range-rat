# Playtest round 1: the playtesters' improvement ideas (input for council round 6)

Three agents played the whole game on 2026-11-24 (A: a new crew's Muni run to the Club; B: Club and
Tiered; C: Stadium → County → Orbital → the Last Bucket → endless). Their bug findings are fixed
(see the "Full-game agent playtest" section of game-design.md). Below are their own ideas for making
it more fun, asked for by the fun council.

## Playtester A — the first night to the Club

**Top 5**
1. **Quota is more than hosing (M).** Today the target is decided purely by Curb at the bell; banking
   doesn't matter. Make it "Bank N balls AND BV $X" so pickers and hosers both earn the stamp.
2. **Live crew progress on the Board (S).** A big BV-vs-target dial whose needle moves as someone
   hoses, plus a one-word sting when it crosses. On a couch everyone sees it and yells "the mats!"
3. **A "last 60 s rally" at the Muni (S).** Last Call is invisible early (no glow balls on day 1).
   Horn, "clean it all before the bell", a crowd cheer at the bell when the quota hits: each day gets
   a climax instead of the report just appearing.
4. **Gary as a crew event (M).** A 3-2-1 huddle countdown on the Board; each ✓/✗ gets a big sound and
   floats where everyone sees it; Gary does a silly salute if the huddle is complete. Already the best
   moment: lean in.
5. **First-night "jobs board" for 4 (M).** On day 2 each Rat gets one suggested job from a pool
   (hose / crank / pick / shop) — a nudge, not an assignment — so friends discover the split.

**Cut/simplify:** the day-1 promotion panel and stamp/fee/quota vocabulary before day 2. Stamps only
once the first is earned; the fee only at 2 stamps.

**Best:** Gary's inspection drive (sign → mats → cart → stand while you huddle, "+2 commendations").
**Flattest:** days 2–3 at the opening bell: BV already beats the target, the stamp arrives unearned.

**Making features meaningful**
- Square ball: give it a decision (doesn't roll so it doesn't spread; reels can't take it, hand only;
  or ×2 if banked unwashed) — or cut it at the Muni.
- Hard hats: also keep your pocket on a knockdown (no spill) and block grime. BONK, balls stay.
- The Muni cart: unlock at the Club, or a cheap "rusty cart" tier ($250, holds 80), and cart unloads
  go straight into the washer at full value when someone is cranking.
- Hit-the-Cart: a big target on the parked cart so the crew can hit it from a bay when nobody drives
  (a small Hustle bonus): a downtime toy at the start of a day.

## Playtester B — the Club and Tiered

**Top 5**
1. **Target Greens you can see and hit (M).** A tall neon pin and a glowing ring on each bowl; a faint
   landing-spot arc while you hold at the bay; widen the timing to ~0.2 s of hold, or snap anything
   within 1 m of the lip into the cup. Today Bowl A is invisible from the bay and the window is
   50–90 ms. A shooting gallery the crew cheers for is the best thing Tiered could have.
2. **The Upper Deck drifts on its own (S).** Loungers order their own drinks every 40–60 s (WOOO, a mug
   raised); the cook's job becomes keeping them under six with hot dogs while beer still pays the tab.
   Today it either never fires or fires every 30 s.
3. **Kitchen orders at 4 players (M).** Replace the invisible stock drain with three visible tickets at
   the shed (fries / hot dog / beer), each paying a little and expiring in ~30 s; Gary's ticket is
   gold; two Rats can cook side by side. A busy diner instead of a quiet leak.
4. **Scuba even cooler (S–M):** lead/gold glint as your light sweeps them; each Lost & Found treasure
   gets a reveal (it spins in the light, the case at the stand lights up); the dock pumper sees a
   bubble trail over their diver; the bag visibly rises past the diver when sent up.
5. **Gutter cups you can watch drain (S):** a glass pipe and clunk-thunk as balls roll to the stand;
   "Cups 3 · 0 · 5" on the Board instead of a percentage.

**Cut/simplify:** the stacked floating cash popups from helpers and the washer (one rolling total per
source, or only your own banks). *(The washer popup was rolled up to one per 2 s after round 1.)*

**Best:** the Upper Deck fall (physical, funny); the underwater caustics a close second.
**Flattest:** standing at the Tiered crew bay looking at a green field with no visible target.

## Playtester C — Stadium → County → Orbital → the Last Bucket → endless

(The "no income" result is from an abstract bot, not real players.)

**Top 5**
1. **Late automation multiplies something (S).** Second cart: double ping value and crowd gain while
   both carts are on the Track. Drones: +25% for the strays they bank ("county fines"). Tether: keeps
   Orbital Debris at 1.0 and banks debris at weight 5. Each buy becomes a visible jump in income.
2. **The venue KPI is a stamp condition (S).** From the Stadium on, the quota also needs the venue
   gauge ≥ 50% at the bell (a second mark on the Board's target bar). Lapping, walking the Yards and
   the EVA become the shift's team play.
3. **Stretch the Orbital into a 3-stamp finale (S).** Leave more balls (~3,500 at 4P instead of
   1,958); customers keep adding orbit debris so the countdown wobbles. The space ride is the best toy
   and gets ~1.5 shifts.
4. **Endless: "buy Site 12 back" (M).** Owner purchases with visible payoffs: repaint the range in the
   crew's colour, a crew statue, grow the field (more mats/balls), a gold cart, name the Jumbotron.
   Field growth raises income so the record can be beaten.
5. **The Jumbotron above the stand roofline and ~2× bigger (M; L for a real replay):** a real 3-s
   knockdown replay (a ghost of the victim falling) instead of the text card.

**Cut/simplify:** "Balls Outstanding" in endless (a live 2–19 means nothing); show "Today vs record".

**Best:** riding the Lift into space: the Earth's limb, giant debris golf balls. No text needed.
**Flattest:** banking Ball No. 1 (now unburied, but still a solo errand). A beat where the whole crew
holds it together at the stand, a confetti cannon, the crowd's roar.
