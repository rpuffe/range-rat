# The owner's ideas (2026-11-24): for Fun Council round 9

The owner has been playing the game on PC and Xbox and gave these ideas during today's rounds. They are
listed here so the council can judge how each fits the early and late game and suggest adjustments.
Status: **built** (live or merged), **branch** (built, waiting to merge), **planned** (spec in progress
in design/owner-playtest-3.md on the `layout` worktree), **open** (no plan yet).

## Already built from earlier owner calls
- Day-1 shared checklist; the shop grows by day; kitchen at the Club; a free teenager on day 2. *(built)*
- One short message on screen at a time; a paced intro. *(built)*
- Signs that face you; real-looking stations (pressure washer, ball washer, dispenser, shed). *(built)*
- The ball washer and snack shed don't exist until bought; one-time tips for new things; mats with mud
  that washes off in patches; spray from the nozzle. *(built)*
- End of the day: the camera lifts overhead, missed balls glow ("N left out there"), the crew walks off.
  *(built)*

## Layout (branch `layout`)
- A **ball return** near the range is where balls are dropped; balls roll by pipe to the **bank / silo**.
- Once bought, the **ball washer** sits on that pipe between the return and the bank.
- **Customers take buckets from a basket** by the dispenser machine.

## From the owner's console playtest (planned)
1. **Ball Value isn't clear.** "All I see is a ball value, a plus something per second, and an upkeep is
   something else." A new player must understand what each ball pays, what raises it (clean grounds),
   and that the daily pass/fail target is about cleanliness. The owner suggested BV could simply be
   based on the cleanliness of the grounds. **(Most important.)**
2. **The end of the day:** glow balls from 1:30 left; customers stop hitting at 1:00 and walk out; the
   last minute is cleanup (pick up the last balls, clean up).
3. **Mats persist overnight** (no re-hosing every morning). The morning gets a new job instead: **fill
   buckets of balls** to stock the dispenser for customers, later automated by an **auto bucket filler**
   upgrade — "we need something to do at the beginning of the day once we have more automations."
4. **Early game feels slow** ("day 6 and still on my very basic round"): keep the cart's pace, but fill
   the bays sooner / more balls earlier in the shift, or make early shifts more worthwhile.
5. **Upgrade the pressure washer** — it's a core mechanic now.
6. **Movement speed and carry capacity upgrades apply to every player** (one purchase for the crew).
7. **Controls:** spray on Right Trigger; sprint on L3 (Call of Duty style); the end-of-day report and
   the shop must work fully on a controller and never knock the player out of full screen.
8. **The cart's full-throttle engine sound** is whiny and grating.
9. Hard hats: the owner hasn't tested them well; leave for now.
